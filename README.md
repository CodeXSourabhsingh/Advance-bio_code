# 🧬 Advance-bio_code

## PRISM: Advanced Clinical Pipeline

PRISM is a compact clinical intelligence app for exploring disease cohorts, biomarker trends, and trial fit in a single workflow. It simulates patient populations, filters them by disease, stage, age, and optional CRISPR mutation, then analyzes biomarker distributions to identify abnormal values and safety alerts. It also fetches relevant clinical trials from ClinicalTrials.gov to connect cohort profiles with real-world study opportunities.

### Core capabilities
- Cohort filtering by disease, stage, age, and mutation
- Biomarker summary statistics and abnormal-range detection
- Outlier tracking for safety review
- Clinical trial lookup by condition
- MySQL-backed logging of pipeline runs

## ⚡ CRISPR Gene Editing Simulator

A bioinformatics tool that fetches gene sequences from NCBI, scans both DNA strands for PAM sites, evaluates gRNA mismatch patterns, simulates Cas9 cleavage, and predicts likely repair outcomes.
